Spinal X-ray Segmentation

A computer vision pipeline that classifies individual vertebrae (T1 to L5) from spinal X-rays, built during a research assistantship at King Chulalongkorn Memorial Hospital.

PythonOpenCVPyTorchOptunaCLAHE
Role Assistant Researcher
Organization King Chulalongkorn Memorial Hospital
Timeline 06.2026 — 08.2026
Stack Python · OpenCV · PyTorch · Optuna · CLAHE

Overview

The goal was to take raw spinal X-ray images and reliably segment and classify each vertebra from T1 down to L5, a task that's normally done manually by radiologists. I focused on the image preprocessing and model-tuning side of the pipeline: getting noisy clinical scans into a state where a segmentation model could actually learn useful boundaries, then squeezing out extra accuracy through systematic hyperparameter search.

Highlights

  • Implemented computer vision pipelines for medical image segmentation of spinal X-rays to classify individual vertebrae (T1 to L5).
  • Enhanced image quality using CLAHE (contrast-limited adaptive histogram equalization) and morphological dilation to make vertebral boundaries easier for the model to learn.
  • Trained rectangular pre-trained models tuned with Optuna hyperparameter optimization to improve segmentation accuracy.
