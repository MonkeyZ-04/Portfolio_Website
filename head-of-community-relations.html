Overview

Before moving into IT, I led community relations for the club's field camps — planning activities around each community's culture and real needs, and acting as the main point of contact between student volunteers and village leaders so the work would actually be welcomed.

Highlights

  • Initiated and planned activities for student volunteers to do together with villagers, analyzing cultural context, ways of life, and the community's real needs to maximize benefit and avoid negative impact.
  • Acted as the main liaison in the field — visiting, talking, and building relationships between the student group and community leaders so the work would be accepted and supported.
  • Handled in-camp communication, drafting announcements and guidelines on conduct, respecting local sites, and cultural precautions for all camp participants.
